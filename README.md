# Muhammad Thashreef — AI & Machine Learning Engineer Portfolio

A futuristic, premium personal portfolio website engineered with **React**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Framer Motion**, adhering to a **Black + Neon Green Futuristic Glassmorphism** design system inspired by high-end AI research labs and developer architectures.

---

## 🎨 Visual Design Specifications

- **Background:** `#050706` (Deep Black)
- **Secondary Background:** `#0B100D` (Dark Surface)
- **Glass Panels:** `rgba(18, 28, 21, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
- **Neon Green:** `#6DFF9B` (Primary Accent & Synaptic Glow)
- **Electric Cyan:** `#00D9FF` (Secondary Accent & Multimodal Highlights)
- **Main Text:** `#F4F7F4`
- **Secondary Text:** `#9AA8A2`
- **Borders:** `rgba(109, 255, 155, 0.18)` (with `0.45` glow on hover)

---

## 🚀 Key Architectural Features

1. **Interactive Neural Network Canvas (`NeuralNetworkCanvas.jsx`):**
   - 3D-depth particle constellation with real-time distance connection algorithms.
   - Mouse gravitation and synaptic transmission pulses (action potentials) firing across nodes in neon green and electric cyan.
2. **Subtle Mouse-Following Ambient Lighting (`MouseSpotlight.jsx`):**
   - Dynamic radial gradient spotlight tracking cursor movement to softly illuminate frosted glass panels.
3. **Hero Section:**
   - Bold typography, status badge (`AIML @ Yenepoya Institute of Technology · Mangaluru, IN`).
   - Floating translucent glass metric chips (Generative AI, AutoML Optuna, DocTR OCR 99.2%, SSE Telemetry).
   - 3 Elegant CTAs: Explore Systems, Open Terminal, Get in Touch.
4. **Two-Column Project Grid with 3D Tilt & Illumination:**
   - Interactive 3D perspective tilt reacting to mouse position with dynamic glare reflection.
   - High-fidelity custom SVG/CSS visual previews (Laser OCR Scanner, Optuna Loss Curve, Voice Audio Waveform).
   - Expandable rich Case Study Modal detailing problem statements, multi-step pipeline architectures, and measured benchmark impact.
5. **Interactive AI Engineering Playground:**
   - **Convert.ai OCR Simulator:** Click to trigger a simulated scanline pass, bounding box extraction, and structured tabular grade sheet generation.
   - **AutoML Studio Optimizer:** Interactive slider controlling Optuna Bayesian trial count, dynamically updating CV accuracy and model selection.
6. **System Diagnostic CLI Shell (`TerminalSection.jsx`):**
   - Interactive hacker terminal supporting commands like `whoami`, `projects`, `convert-ai`, `automl`, `jarvis`, `skills`, `edu`, `contact`, and `clear`.
7. **Technical Skills Arsenal:**
   - 4 categorized matrices (AI & ML, Languages, Full-Stack & Systems, CS Foundations) with animated proficiency bars and development toolchain pills.
8. **Education & Certifications:**
   - Academic timeline at Yenepoya Institute of Technology (2023–2027) + verified certifications from MongoDB University, IIT Bombay (Git), and Simplilearn (NLP & AI).
9. **Contact & Encrypted Dispatch:**
   - Direct email copying, phone link, verified GitHub/LinkedIn profiles, and immediate availability status.
